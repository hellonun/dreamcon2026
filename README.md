# Dream Con Fair 2026 · Space Composer

A 3D model of the BACC Level L foyer for planning the Dream Con Fair 2026 exhibition (1–6 Dec 2026).

- **Space**: the foyer circle traced from the BACC plan, with columns, stairs, walls, the 2.70 m ceiling and zones.
- **Scaffold**: compose the exhibition wall from scaffold bays; drag, rotate, resize and stack pieces.
- **Prints**: dress the bays with canvas prints, including graphics that span several bays.
- **Bricks**: a round scaffold platform with a CNC-cut plywood top and holes to sit in, where 1000 bricks become a landscape over six days, with the message input station and the vertical screen.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site. Everything is static: three.js loads from jsDelivr and the images are in `assets/`.

Work is saved in your browser. Use **Save / load** in the app to copy a composition out or bring one back.
